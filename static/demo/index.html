InvoiceFlow

Accounts payable teams need to see whether an invoice agrees with the purchase order before anyone treats it as ready. This page runs those checks on three synthetic invoices.

Try a sample

Choose an example. The server allows only these three identifiers. Results were computed when this demo process started, from project fixtures. A click shows that stored result and does not insert another invoice.

Clean invoice

INV-1001 bills Northstar Office Supply purchase order PO-1001 for the ordered quantities and unit prices.

Expected outcome: No issues found by the implemented checks.

Quantity mismatch

INV-1003 bills 5 units of toner. PO-1001 ordered 6. The invoice line totals agree with its own quantities and prices.

Expected outcome: a quantity mismatch and a purchase-order total mismatch.

Unit price mismatch

INV-1002 bills paper at a higher unit price than PO-1001. The invoice line totals agree with its own quantities and prices.

Expected outcome: a unit-price mismatch and a purchase-order total mismatch.

    How this demo works
    
        
      
          
    1. The page sends an allowlisted sample id. It does not send invoice fields or a file.
      2. 
          
    2. At startup the demo loads synthetic vendors and purchase orders into a fresh in-memory database and runs the existing checks once.
      3. 
          
    3. Validation confirms the invoice shape. Matching finds the vendor and purchase order. Reconciliation compares quantities, prices, and totals.
      4. 
          
    4. The browser renders the stored result. PDF extraction and the model call are not on this path.
      5. 
        
    
        
    
          Text-based PDF extraction exists in the separate local prototype. It
          is disabled on this public website. This page does not upload
          documents or call an AI model.
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Limitations
    
        
      
          
    • The invoices, vendors, and purchase orders are synthetic.
      • 
          
    • A status of matched means the vendor and purchase order were found and the checks ran. It does not mean the invoice is approved or safe to pay.
      • 
          
    • There is no human approval workflow, OCR, payment execution, or customer invoicing product on this website.
      • 
          
    • The checks cover this prototype's rules only: one purchase order, whole-order billing, and no tax, freight, or receipts.
      • 
          
    • The request limit is in-memory and applies to this single process. It is not distributed rate limiting and it is not DDoS protection.
      • 
          
    • Restarting the process rebuilds the demo database from fixtures.
      • 
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Privacy
    
        
    
          The application processes only the curated sample id. It does not
          store a new invoice for each visit. The server log can still record
          the request method, path, status, and client address. A host or
          reverse proxy may retain connection metadata, including IP addresses.
          API responses are sent with a no-store cache directive.
          This note describes the demo's behavior. It is not legal advice.
